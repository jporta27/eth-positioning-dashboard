## Flag-emergente findings (strict 4h rule)

No regime combinations crossed the strict edge bar at the 4h horizon.

Two reads:
1. The aggregate funding/stables signals genuinely have no 4h edge —
   slicing on regime doesn't manufacture one.
2. The 4h horizon may simply be too short for these mean-reverting
   signals. The cross-horizon table above shows several combos where
   CI95 *does* exclude 0 at 1d / 3d / 7d (notably funding_hot_outflow
   conditioned on btc_trend_30d=btc_down at 3d/7d — IC>+0.4, p<0.01).
   DSR pinned at 0 across the board because Bailey & López de Prado's
   deflation against n_trials=10 with a per-event Sharpe ~0.3 is
   numerically very harsh; the Sharpe variance term blows up under
   small-sample non-normality. This is a known limitation of DSR on
   small n; for these findings to clear, either n must grow or a
   pre-registered single-strategy test (n_trials=1) is more appropriate.
